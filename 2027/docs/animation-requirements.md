# NBA 2K27 Best Animations Guide — By Height & Category

Best animations to equip per category, organized by height bracket, with the
attribute requirement so you can plan a build around them.

> Sources: nba2klab.com (Koza2K tested rankings, Aug 2026), lockercodes.io
> animation requirements (checked Sep 3, 2026). Requirement data is verified;
> "best" picks for size-ups come from Koza2K's scored testing.

---

## How 2K27 animations are graded

nba2klab partnered with Koza2K to test **735 dribble moves across 30 move types**
at each height break. Key scoring:

- **Dribble Style** — gated by **Speed With Ball**; graded on Speedboost + Cross Launch
- **Signature Size-Up** — gated by **Ball Handle**; graded on Crossover + Between
  the Legs + Behind the Back
- Most other move types — Ball Handle, single overall grade

2K27 split 2K26's 10 dribble categories into **30**, so crossovers alone now break
into Crossover / Crossover Escape / Crossover Stepback / Misdirection Crossover,
each ranked separately. The fine-grained per-move rankings are behind nba2klab
Premium; the requirement tables below are free/verified.

## 6'5"–6'9" (Wings / Forwards)

You keep almost all guard animations (most run to 6'9), but **lose the ≤6'4-only
elite dribble styles** (Kyrie, Curry, Trae, Lillard, Westbrook). Your best dribble
styles shift to the ≤6'9 group.

### Dribble Style (Speed With Ball)

| # | Animation | SWB Req | Height | Why |
|---|-----------|--------:|--------|-----|
| 1 | **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** | 84 | ≤6'9 | Best available at wing height |
| 2 | Zach LaVine | 83 | ≤6'9 | Smooth, explosive |
| 3 | Tyrese Maxey | 82 | ≤6'9 | Fast, accessible |
| 4 | Chris Paul / Luka Doncic / Donovan Mitchell | 80 | ≤6'9 | Control-oriented |
| 5 | Amen Thompson | 79 | 6'5–6'9 | Wing-specific, athletic |

### Signature Size-Up (Ball Handle)

Same top group as guards (Harden, SGA, Kawhi, Coby, Tatum all run to 6'9):
- 🏆 **James Harden** (86 BH) — still the best, works to 6'9
- **Jayson Tatum** (75 BH) or **Kawhi Leonard** (80 BH) — best value at wing

### Big-wing exclusive styles (6'5+)
- Kevin Durant (65 SWB) — tall-specific
- Zion Williamson (60 SWB, 6'5+) — power handle

## 6'10"+ (Bigs / Point Center)

Guard handle animations are gone; you get the **big-man dribble styles**. This is
the relevant set for the 6'10 point center in `build-minimums-guide.md`.

### Dribble Style (Speed With Ball) — big-man options

| # | Animation | SWB Req | Height | Why |
|---|-----------|--------:|--------|-----|
| 1 | **Jaren Jackson Jr** | 69 | 6'10+ | Highest SWB req of the bigs = most fluid |
| 2 | Victor Wembanyama | 68 | 6'10+ | Long, mobile handle for a 7-footer |
| 3 | Chet Holmgren | 60 | 6'10+ | Good value at 60 SWB |
| 4 | Nikola Jokic | 50 | 6'10+ | Point-center feel, low SWB |
| 5 | Paolo Banchero | 50 | 6'10+ | Forward-style at 50 |
| — | Domantas Sabonis | 40 | 6'10+ | Cheapest usable |

For a **point center**, the JJJ or Wembanyama style (68–69 SWB) is worth the small
investment — it's the smoothest handle a 6'10 can equip, and it pairs with the
90 Ball Handle floor from the build guide.

### Size-Up at 6'10
Most signature size-ups **cap at 6'9**, so a 6'10 build has **very limited size-up
access** — this is a real cost of the height. Confirm in the Builder which size-ups
remain available at exactly 6'10 before committing Ball Handle points expecting a
guard-style size-up; you may be limited to basic/big-man packages.

## Key principles

1. **Height gates cut hardest at the extremes.** ≤6'4 unlocks the elite handle
   styles (Kyrie/Curry/Trae); 6'10+ loses guard size-ups almost entirely. A 6'5–6'9
   wing keeps the widest usable set.
2. **Match the animation req to your build's actual attribute** — no point owning a
   94-SWB Kyrie style on an 80-SWB build; you can't equip it.
3. **Value beats prestige.** Jayson Tatum size-up (75 BH) ties the 90+ options on
   Koza2K's score — buy the cheap one and spend the saved points elsewhere.
4. **For the point center:** budget ~68 SWB for a JJJ/Wemby dribble style, and
   verify size-up availability at 6'10 before assuming guard-style handles.

> **Confidence note:** requirement data verified (lockercodes, Sep 3). The ranked
> "best" ordering for dribble styles/crossovers/dunks/jumpers beyond size-ups is on
> nba2klab Premium (Koza2K testing) and isn't fully reproduced here — this doc
> covers requirements + the free size-up rankings. 2K27 is new; expect animation
> tuning in patches.

### The gate pattern (applies to ALL move-types)

Every dribble move-type uses the same tiering — so once you know your Ball Handle and
height, you know roughly what you can equip in **any** category (hesi, spin, stepback,
escapes, combos included):

| Your build | Ball Handle | Best move-type tier you unlock |
|------------|------------:|--------------------------------|
| **Elite guard ≤6'4** | 90–92 | Curry / Trae / Kyrie / Lillard — the glitchiest in every category |
| **Playmaking guard/wing ≤6'9** | 86–91 | SGA (91), Harden (86), Ja/Poole (86) — top wing-legal moves |
| **2-way / balanced ≤6'9** | 80–85 | Chris Paul / Kawhi / LeBron (80) — great value tier |
| **Low-BH wing ≤6'9** | 75 | MJ / Tatum / DeRozan (75) — solid, cheap |
| **Point center / big 6'10+** | 68–75 | **Wemby (68)**, Paolo (75), Sabonis (75) — the only bigs with real handle moves |

**For the 6'10 point center:** at 6'10+ your crossover/behind-back options collapse to
the **big-only** entries — **Wembanyama (68 BH)** is the best-value handle move, Paolo
(75) the best if you can afford it. Guard moves (Curry, Kyrie, etc.) are **hard-locked
out by height**, no matter your Ball Handle. This is the same size-up cliff noted above.
