# NBA 2K27 Dunk Guide — By Rating Tier & Height

Which dunk packages to equip based on your Driving Dunk / Standing Dunk, Vertical,
and height. Pick the tier your build can actually reach — the game will not offer
higher-tier animations no matter how much VC you carry.

> Sources: lockercodes.io dunk requirements (all 147 packages, verified Sep 3,
> 2026), nerdschalk.com. Requirement data is verified from the game's tables.

---

## How dunk unlocks work in 2K27

Every dunk package has a **Driving Dunk (DD)** or **Standing Dunk (SD)** minimum,
a **Vertical (Vert)** minimum, and a **height range**. Key facts:

- **Driving Dunk = moving dunks** (off one/two feet, in traffic). **Standing Dunk =
  under-basket / big-man dunks** from a standstill.
- A **high Driving Dunk in the 90s** opens the flashy, contact, and signature tiers.
  A build in the **70s–low 80s will not see those animations offered at all.**
- **Contact dunks** are the strongest (trigger posterizers) and gate on both a high
  DD/SD *and* a high Vertical.
- Guards (≤6'4) get **small contact** variants at slightly lower reqs; bigs (6'10+)
  get **standing/bigman** contact and alley-oops.

## Build-specific recommendations

**6'10 Point Center (the build in `build-minimums-guide.md`):**
- Your Standing Dunk floor there was ~90 for Gold badge tier → you can reach
  **Elite Bigman Contact Dunks (SD 90, Vert 70)** or **Victor Wembanyama sig**.
- Equip **Bigman Contact Alley-Oop (70 DD / 85 SD)** for lob finishes off your own
  passing — you're the roll man *and* the passer.
- If Vertical is short of 70, drop to **Pro Bigman Contact (SD 80, Vert 60)** — still
  a contact dunk, just needs less hops.

**Slasher / high-flyer guard (≤6'9):**
- Chase **DD 89+** for Pro Contact Dunks (cheapest contact) or push to 93 for Small
  Contact (≤6'4) / 99 for Elite Contact.
- Pair with **Ja Morant** or **Russell Westbrook** sigs.

**Balanced / 2-way (didn't invest in dunk):**
- DD 80 gets you Clutch Reverses + most player sigs (LeBron, MJ, T-Mac) — plenty
  flashy without overspending.

## Key principles

1. **Contact dunks need BOTH a high dunk rating AND high Vertical.** A 90 DD with a
   60 Vert still won't unlock Elite Contact (needs 90 Vert). Budget both.
2. **The 90 Driving Dunk cliff is real** — below it, flashy/contact/best-sig tiers
   simply aren't offered. Decide before capping whether you want to clear it.
3. **Guards get small-contact variants ≤6'4** at lower reqs (93 DD / 86 DD) — a
   short slasher can posterize without a 99.
4. **Bigs dunk off Standing Dunk, not Driving Dunk.** Don't waste points on Driving
   Dunk for a 6'10+ paint big; SD + Vert is what unlocks your posters and lobs.
5. **Vertical does double duty** — it feeds dunk unlocks *and* rebounding/block
   badges (see `build-minimums-guide.md`), so it's efficient for a point center.
