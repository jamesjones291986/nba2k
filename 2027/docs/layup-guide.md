# NBA 2K27 Layup Guide — By Height & Driving Layup

Which layup packages your build can equip, gated by **Driving Layup (DL)** and
**height**. Layups are your bread-and-butter finish when a dunk isn't there — every
build uses them. Pick the best one your DL + height unlocks.

> Sources: lockercodes.io layup requirements (all 80 packages, verified Sep 3,
> 2026). Requirement data is verified from the game's tables. "Best" ordering is by
> requirement + known animation quality (release speed / hang time), not a lab
> layup-make test.

---

## How layup packages work in 2K27

- Every package gates on a **Driving Layup** minimum **and a height range.**
- Unlike dunks, **height ranges are tighter** — most packages are locked to one of
  three bands: **≤6'4 (small)**, **6'5–6'9 (swing)**, or **6'10+ (big)**. A few span
  wider. This is why a guard and a big literally cannot use each other's layups.
- **Default Small / Default Big / WNBA** packages need 0 DL — always available as a
  fallback.
- Higher DL doesn't just unlock packages, it also improves layup **make % and
  contact finishing** via badges (Float Game, Ghost Stepper — see
  `badge-requirements.md`).

**For the point center:** your Target build carries **~85 Driving Layup** (the
2-Way Bucket Chaser had 85) — that unlocks **Paolo Banchero**, the best big package,
which finishes with a guard-like smoothness that fits a point-center's game. If your
DL is lower (~55–60 on a pure rebounder build), you're into Wemby/Sabonis tier or
Default Big — still functional, just less craft.

## Key principles

1. **Layup packages are height-band locked** — ≤6'4, 6'5–6'9, or 6'10+. You cannot
   equip a guard package on a big, so don't plan around one.
2. **Higher DL = better package AND better finishing badges** (Float Game, Ghost
   Stepper). It does double duty, so it's rarely wasted on a slasher.
3. **Value picks per band:** Trae/Garland (80) for guards, Zion (70) for wings,
   Wemby/Sabonis (70–78) for bigs — all near-top quality below the 85+ elite tier.
4. **Point center:** ~85 DL unlocks Paolo Banchero (best big package). Below that,
   Wemby/Sabonis are fine — layups matter less than your standing-dunk finishing.
